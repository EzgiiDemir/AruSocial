NAME / AD: Arkin University of Creative Arts and Design
  = Arkın Yaratıcı Sanatlar ve Tasarım Üniversitesi = ARUCAD
FOUNDED / KURULUŞ: 2017 (teaching began 2018–2019 / eğitime 2018–2019'da başladı)
TYPE / TÜR: non-profit foundation university / vakıf üniversitesi
COUNTRY / ÜLKE: Northern Cyprus, TRNC / Kuzey Kıbrıs, KKTC
MAIN CAMPUS / ANA KAMPÜS: Kyrenia / Girne
RECTOR / REKTÖR: Prof. Dr. Asım Vehbi
CHAIR OF TRUSTEES / MÜTEVELLİ HEYETİ BAŞKANI: Yrd. Doç. Dr. Sinan Arkın
LANGUAGE / EĞİTİM DİLİ: mainly English; some programmes in Turkish
  (e.g. New Media and Communication, Acting). Varies by programme.

MOTTO: "Learn while producing, produce while learning."
  = "Üreterek öğrenmek, öğrenerek üretmek."

CHARACTER: a thematic university for art, design and communication.
  Practice-based: workshops, studios, laboratories, projects, exhibitions,
  performances. Not a conventional general-purpose university.

FACULTIES: EXACTLY FOUR (4) / FAKÜLTE SAYISI: TAM DÖRT (4)
FACULTIES AND UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAMMES / FAKÜLTELER VE LİSANS PROGRAMLARI
- Faculty of Arts / Sanat Fakültesi: Plastic Arts, Ceramics, Photography,
  Archaeology, Film Design and Directing, Textile and Fashion Design
- Faculty of Design / Tasarım Fakültesi: Architecture, Interior Architecture
  and Environmental Design, Industrial Design, Urban Design and Landscape
  Architecture
- Faculty of Communication / İletişim Fakültesi: Digital Game Design,
  New Media and Communication, Visual Communication Design
- Faculty of Music and Performing Arts / Müzik ve Sahne Sanatları Fakültesi:
  Acting, Modern Dance, Sound Arts and Design
- Also: School of English Preparatory Studies, Vocational School,
  Institute of Graduate Education and Research

CAMPUSES / KAMPÜSLER
- Kyrenia main campus: Şair Nedim Street No:11, Kyrenia, TRNC (Mersin 10 Turkey)
- Bandabuliya Campus: Uray Street No:14, Walled City, Nicosia
- Workshops Building: İskenderun Street, Kyrenia
- ARUCAD Art Space: Müftü Raci Efendi Street No:48, Nicosia

LIBRARY / KÜTÜPHANE: founded 2017, new building since 2022. Collection centred
  on art, communication, design, philosophy, cinema, architecture, ceramics,
  photography. Has study rooms and a Digital Library laboratory.

ADMISSION / ÖĞRENCİ KABULÜ: varies by programme. Special-talent examination
  may apply to Plastic Arts, Ceramics, Textile and Fashion Design,
  Photography, Acting, Modern Dance, Sound Arts and Design — assessed by
  drawing exam, portfolio, or audition depending on the department.
  Applicants from Turkey may enter some departments through YKS/ÖSYM.

RECOGNITION / TANINIRLIK: programmes are approved by YÖK; Turkish equivalency
  is handled in that framework. Graduates receive a Diploma Supplement.
  Accreditation is per programme and must not be generalised. Verified
  example: New Media and Communication and Visual Communication Design were
  accredited by İLAD after İLEDAK evaluation, 9 March 2026 – 9 March 2031.
