ARUCAD is a community for students to communicate, exchange ideas, and support one another. These guidelines apply to posts, comments, messages, profile information, and all other activity within the app.

## 1. Treat Others With Respect

You may disagree with other users, but you must communicate respectfully. Harassment, humiliation, intimidation, targeted abuse, and repeated unwanted contact are not permitted.

## 2. Keep the Community Safe

Do not publish or promote:

- Threats, violence, or encouragement of self-harm
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Bullying, harassment, or sexual harassment
- Sexually explicit or exploitative material
- Dangerous, illegal, or seriously harmful activities
- Spam, scams, malicious links, or deceptive content

If someone appears to be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or university security.

## 3. Respect Privacy

Do not share another person's private or identifying information without permission. This includes contact details, private messages, student records, photographs, and confidential documents.

Do not record or distribute private conversations without the required consent.

## 4. Respect Intellectual Property

Only share content that you created or have permission to use. Do not copy, distribute, or claim ownership of another person's work without authorization or proper attribution.

## 5. Be Authentic

Do not impersonate another person, create misleading identities, manipulate engagement, or intentionally spread false information that may cause harm.

## 6. Academic and University Responsibilities

Do not use the app to facilitate academic misconduct, disclose confidential university information, or interfere with university activities.

Use of the app remains subject to applicable university policies and regulations.

## 7. Reporting and Moderation

Users can report content or behavior that may violate these guidelines. Reports may be assessed using automated safety systems and reviewed by authorized moderators.

Depending on the nature and seriousness of a violation, we may:

- Remove or restrict content
- Issue a warning
- Limit certain account features
- Temporarily suspend or permanently disable an account
- Preserve relevant records
- Refer the matter to university authorities or legally authorized institutions

Enforcement decisions may consider the context, severity, frequency, and potential harm of the conduct.

## 8. Appeals

If action is taken against your content or account, you may request a review through [appeal method or email]. Submitting an appeal does not guarantee that the decision will be changed.

## 9. Updates

These guidelines may be updated to reflect changes to the app, community needs, university regulations, or legal requirements. Significant changes will be communicated to users.
