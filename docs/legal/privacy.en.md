ARUCAD respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, and how we protect it when you use the app.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

- Your name, university email address, student ID, and profile information
- Posts, comments, messages, reports, and other content you submit
- Technical information such as device type, IP address, app version, and activity logs
- Information you provide when contacting support or reporting content

Please do not share sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

- Create and manage your account
- Provide the app's communication and community features
- Protect users and maintain a safe environment
- Detect spam, abuse, security threats, and violations
- Review reported content and respond to complaints
- Improve the performance and usability of the app
- Comply with legal and university requirements

## 3. Content Moderation

Content shared through the app may be checked by automated safety systems. Reported or potentially harmful content may also be reviewed by authorized moderators.

When necessary, serious violations may be referred to the relevant university authorities or legally authorized institutions.

Automated systems help identify possible violations, but important enforcement decisions may also involve human review.

## 4. When Information May Be Shared

We may share limited information:

- With authorized moderators and university personnel
- With service providers that help us operate and secure the app
- When required by law or a valid legal request
- To protect the rights, safety, and security of users or others
- During the investigation of serious Community Guidelines violations

We do not sell your personal data.

## 5. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve complaints, meet university requirements, or comply with the law.

Some records may be retained after account deletion when required for safety, disciplinary, or legal purposes.

## 6. Your Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable laws, you may have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of certain information
- Object to or restrict certain processing activities
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- Submit a privacy complaint

Some requests may be limited when information must be retained for legal, security, or disciplinary reasons.

## 7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal data. However, no digital service can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for protecting your login information and notifying us if you believe your account has been compromised.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, university procedures, or legal requirements change. We will notify users when a significant update requires attention or renewed consent.
